# Opportunity Scorecard

Scoring is 1–10 per criterion, informed by the research in `MARKET.md`. These are **judgment calls grounded in evidence**, not measured data — see `DECISIONS.md` for how each score was reasoned.

## Candidates evaluated

1. **TikTok Shop Appeal Rescue** — productized service: diagnose a seller's violation/suspension, tell them exactly what's missing, and deliver a customized appeal letter + evidence checklist within 24–48h.
2. **Multi-marketplace suspension service (Amazon+Etsy+TikTok)** — same idea, broader surface.
3. **Generic TikTok Shop appeal template pack** — static PDF/doc, no diagnosis, sold once on Etsy/Gumroad.
4. **TikTok Shop analytics/research SaaS** — compete with FindNiche/Kalodata/Kixmon.
5. **Vertical micro-SaaS for local service businesses** (scheduling/invoicing niche tool).

## Selection: TikTok Shop Appeal Rescue

**Winner.** It scores highest on the metrics that matter most for a 3–6 week, $10K objective: urgency, pain, proven willingness-to-pay-by-analogy (Amazon services charge $750–$1,495 for the same job), thin direct competition at an affordable price point, fast speed-to-revenue (can sell before building any software — a manual/AI-assisted service, not an app), near-zero recurring cost, and high margin.

**Why not #2 (multi-marketplace):** Broader isn't better yet — spreading across 3 policy systems (Amazon, Etsy, TikTok) triples the research/positioning work before a single sale, and Amazon's niche is already crowded with established law firms. Start narrow on TikTok Shop where competition is weaker; expand to Amazon/Etsy only once TikTok Shop proves the model (see `DECISIONS.md`).

**Why not #3 (template pack):** Fast and cheap to build, but low price (~$15–29 observed on Etsy) means ~350–670 units needed for $10K — an unrealistic distribution ask in 3–6 weeks with no existing audience. Commodity, no differentiation, easily copied.

**Why not #4/#5:** Both require real software development before any revenue is possible (violates "speed to first revenue"), face entrenched/funded competitors (#4) or slow B2B sales cycles (#5), and neither showed the same evidence of urgent, already-monetized pain.

## What must be true for this to hit $10K (tracked in `10K-PATH.md`)

- Sellers will pay $99–$299 for a fast, specific, non-law-firm appeal service.
- Seller communities (Reddit, Facebook groups) allow or tolerate this kind of offer, or DM outreach works as an alternative.
- One operator (using AI-assisted analysis, not manual legal expertise) can produce a credible, policy-specific appeal in under an hour of hands-on time per order.

These are validated or killed in Week 1 per `DECISIONS.md` kill criteria — not assumed.

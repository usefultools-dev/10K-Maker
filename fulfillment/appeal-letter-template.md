# Appeal Letter Template

**Root Cause**
[1–3 sentences: what specifically happened, referencing the exact violation TikTok cited. Use the customer's own facts from Q3/Q7 — do not speculate beyond what they told us.]

**Immediate Actions Taken**
[Bulleted list of concrete steps already completed — pulled from Q8 and the relevant playbook's evidence checklist. Every claim here should be backed by an attached document/screenshot.]

**Preventive Measures**
[1–3 sentences describing the ongoing process change that prevents recurrence — this is the section most generic appeals skip; make it concrete (e.g., "implemented a pre-listing compliance checklist reviewed before every new product upload" rather than "we will be more careful").]

**Supporting Documentation Attached**
[List each attached file/screenshot by name, matching the evidence checklist for this violation category.]

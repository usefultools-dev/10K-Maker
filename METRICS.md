Log every touch here (or in a simple spreadsheet) starting Day 1 of outreach. Without this, we cannot tell whether the bottleneck is traffic, positioning, price, or trust — and per the operating loop (master-prompt §29), we must diagnose before changing anything.

## Diagnostic loop
- **Replies but no conversations** → positioning/offer problem, fix messaging.
- **Conversations but no sales** → price/trust/proof problem (consider testimonials, guarantee language, lower initial price).
- **No replies at all** → channel/reach problem (wrong communities, banned self-promo, need a different channel like direct outreach to public posters instead of general posts).
- **Sales but slow** → this is working, scale the exact channel/message that produced them; do not diversify yet (principle #46).

Update the dashboard block above after every session of outreach — it is the single source of truth for whether we are closer to $10,000.

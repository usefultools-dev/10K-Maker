# Marketing — TikTok Shop Appeal Rescue

## Target customer
US-based TikTok Shop seller, shop/listing suspended or violated in the last few days, earning meaningful revenue from it (not a hobby seller), currently confused/frustrated by TikTok's generic responses.

## Positioning
"The fast, affordable second opinion on your TikTok Shop suspension — not a $1,500 law firm, not a generic template. A specific diagnosis and a ready-to-submit appeal in 24–48 hours."

## Offer / USP / objections
- **USP:** TikTok-Shop-specific (not a generalist Amazon firm), personalized (not a generic template), instant, and ~20-40x cheaper than legal-service alternatives ($39 vs $750–$1,495).
- **Objections & responses:**
  - *"Is this legal advice?"* → No — explicitly administrative/appeal-writing help, not legal representation. State this upfront.
  - *"Will this guarantee reinstatement?"* → No guarantee is offered; TikTok makes the final call. We guarantee the best possible appeal, fast. (Consider a "if we can't produce a customized diagnosis, full refund" guarantee to reduce risk.)
  - *"Why not just use ChatGPT myself?"* → Generic AI doesn't know TikTok Seller Center's specific appeal requirements per violation type or what evidence actually gets appeals approved — this is targeted, not generic.
  - *"Why should I trust an unknown provider?"* → Early customers get a lower "founding customer" price in exchange for a testimonial; be transparent that this is new.

## Channel priority (per master-prompt §25: speed, intent, cost, accessibility, conversion)
1. **Direct replies to public "my TikTok Shop got suspended" posts** (Reddit, Facebook groups) — highest intent, zero cost, immediate. Start here.
2. **General posts in seller communities** offering the service — secondary, check each group's self-promotion rules first (many ban it; ask mods or find a "services/promo" thread if one exists).
3. **Short-form video (TikTok/YouTube)** showing a real (anonymized) before/after diagnosis — build once organic replies validate the offer; strong fit since the audience already lives on TikTok.

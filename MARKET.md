# Market Research Log

Research date: 2026-09-01. All figures are third-party estimates from the cited sources, not verified primary data — treated as **strong evidence**, not fact, per the no-fake-certainty rule.

## 1. The pain: marketplace account suspensions/violations

Sellers on every major marketplace face account-health violations, listing takedowns, and suspensions that threaten their income, and existing recourse is slow, opaque, and adversarial:

- **TikTok Shop**: Sellers describe violation explanations as "non-existent" and appeals as falling "on deaf ears," with support often limited to bot responses. ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/mindlily/tiktok-shop-got-banned-heres-what-you-need-to-do-1827), [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/four-months-waiting-for-appeal-review/398943)) Sellers get only 2 appeal attempts and must submit violation-specific evidence (root cause, corrective action, preventive measures) via Seller Center. ([e-cabilly.com](https://e-cabilly.com/blog/tiktok-shop-suspension-appeal-recovery-guide/), [galliumlaw.com](https://galliumlaw.com/guides/tiktok-shop-suspended-for-ip-violation/))
- **Amazon**: Same pattern, but the market has matured into paid professional services: AMZ Sellers Attorney charges **$750/month** for a prevention+appeal retainer; The Appeal Guru charges **$1,495/appeal** with a partial refund guarantee; Seller Candy offers a free audit as a lead magnet. ([amazonsellers.attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/amazon-suspension-prevention-plan.html), [theappealguru.com](https://theappealguru.com/), [sellercandy.com](https://sellercandy.com/amazon-suspension-appeal-service)) This proves **real, meaningful willingness to pay** for suspension help — the category is commercially validated on Amazon.
- **Etsy**: Similar suspension/reinstatement pain (unpaid fees, complaints, incomplete verification); third-party reinstatement services exist but the market looks thinner and lower-value than Amazon/TikTok. ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/etsy-account-suspended/), [ShieldMyShop](https://www.shieldmyshop.com/blog/2026-04-01-etsy-account-suspended-cant-reach-support))

## 2. TikTok Shop scale

- US TikTok Shop merchants grew from ~4,450 (mid-2023) to **475,000+ shops** by mid-2025, ~500,000+ by 2026 — the fastest seller growth of any major commerce platform. ([Redstag Fulfillment](https://redstagfulfillment.com/how-many-tiktok-shop-sellers/), [Ringly](https://www.ringly.io/blog/tiktok-shop-statistics-2026))
- ~15 million sellers globally (2025 estimate). Average seller monthly revenue ~$4,200; 23% consistently exceed $5K/month — i.e. a meaningful share of sellers have enough at stake that losing the account is a real financial event, not a hobby loss. ([Dashboardly](https://www.dashboardly.io/statistics/tiktok-shop-seller-statistics))
- Revenue is concentrated at the top (top 1% earn 180x the median), so the addressable "has real money on the line" segment is a subset of all sellers, not all 500K — still plausibly tens of thousands of US sellers with $1K+/month at stake at any given time.

## 3. Competitive landscape for TikTok Shop appeal help specifically

- Existing help is either **free/generic** (TikTok's own help center, DIY blog guides, cheap Etsy PDF template bundles for appeal letters — commodity, no diagnosis) or **expensive/generalist** (Amazon-suspension law firms like AMZ Sellers Attorney and Gallium Law that bolt on TikTok Shop appeals at legal-service prices, $750-$1,500+).
- No affordable, TikTok-Shop-specific, self-serve or light-touch mid-market product was found (roughly $49–$299) that diagnoses the violation, tells the seller exactly what evidence is missing, and drafts the appeal. **This is the gap.**
- Existing TikTok Shop SaaS tools (FindNiche, Kalodata, Kixmon, Dashboardly, $12–$99/mo) are all product-research/analytics tools, not account-health/appeal tools — different job-to-be-done, so not direct competitors.

## 4. Distribution channels observed

- Active Reddit communities (r/TikTokShop and similar), multiple Facebook groups ("TikTok Shop Sellers Group," "TikTok Shop US-Seller," etc.), and TikTok's own seller university content. Seller complaints about suspensions surface organically and frequently in these spaces — i.e., the customer is findable and already talking about the exact pain, in public, right now. ([Facebook groups list](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1398033077448148/))
- Group membership sizes weren't directly measurable via search (Facebook doesn't expose counts to non-members), so channel *reach* is an assumption to validate in week 1 by joining and observing directly — flagged in DECISIONS.md.

## 5. Key uncertainties (assumptions, not facts)

- Whether sellers will pay a stranger (not a law firm) $99–$299 for TikTok-Shop-specific help, absent brand trust — untested until first real outreach.
- Whether cold posting in seller Facebook groups/Reddit is allowed (many groups ban self-promotion) — needs direct observation of group rules in week 1.
- True volume of *new* suspensions per week/month (a proxy for repeat market size) is not available from search; inferred qualitatively from complaint frequency, not measured.
